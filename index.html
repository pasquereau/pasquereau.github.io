About

I am a formal linguist working on semantics and morphosyntax. I use the tools of generative grammar and formal semantics to analyse the grammars of understudied languages, especially Seri (Mexico) and languages of the Caucasus.

Since 2022 I have been a CNRS research scientist at the Laboratoire de Linguistique de Nantes. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Surrey Morphology Group (2017–2021), working on the verbal morphosemantics of plurality in Seri. My PhD at UMass Amherst (2017), supervised by Rajesh Bhatt and Vincent Homer, was on the syntax and semantics of embedded polar response particles, ellipsis and contrast. I started out in linguistics as a master's student at Université Lumière Lyon 2, working on Karata with Denis Creissels and Colette Grinevald.

Language isolate · Sonora, Mexico Data collected in El Desemboque from 2017, then remotely

On verbal and nominal number, pluractionality, distributive numerals and paradigm structure.

For the community: Ihamoquixp Quizjoj, an ABC book in Seri illustrated by Seri children, with the tale “The Milky Way Bearers” (2019, with Debora Perales and Gabriel Hofherr).

Karata

Andic · East Caucasian · Daghestan, Russia Data collected in Daghestan from 2011, then remotely

On valency, spatial cases, questions, reduplication and tense–aspect.
